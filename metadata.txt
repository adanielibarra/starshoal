about=StarShoal downloads Sentinel-2 imagery from the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, prepares masked surface reflectance and estimates shallow-water depth with Stumpf log-ratio, Lyzenga multiband and Random Forest, calibrated and validated on the same points (spatial blocks). Includes Hedley sunglint correction and area masking by polygon. One window with tabs, in Spanish and English; every tool is also in the Processing Toolbox. Outputs depth and trust rasters plus an HTML accuracy report. Optical SDB only works in clear, shallow water.
